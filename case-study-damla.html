Damla Group: a website for a hospitality group

Designing the website of a company that manages hotels, runs event venues and food and beverage concepts, and advises investors.

View live site ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Role
Sole UX/UI designer
Client
Damla Group, Brussels
Scope
Website, desktop and mobile
Period
February to April 2026, live
01

Overview

Damla Group provides in-house hotel management, creates hotel and food and beverage concepts, runs event venues, and guides investors toward real estate opportunities. It works across Belgium and the UK, with offices in Brussels and London.

I was the only designer on the project and designed the whole website, which is now live. It presents the company to hotel owners, investors and future team members: the home page, About, Projects, Careers, Contact and the blog, for desktop and mobile.

02

Structure

The navigation stays short: Home, About, Projects and Careers, with Contact Us as the one highlighted action. The home page introduces the company in order: what it does, its philosophy, then three services (Concept Creation, Hotel Asset Management, Investments & Development), each followed by its own section with photography.

Projects are grouped the way the company works: Hotels, Event Venues and Food & Beverage. Each property is an image card with its name and city, so visitors can scan the portfolio before opening anything.

03

Visual language

The photography of the properties leads. A deep navy for the header and footer, warm beige section backgrounds and a single bronze accent for actions give the site a calm, hospitality feel. Cards use soft shadows and icons in circles, and every page ends with the same call to action and footer.

The pages

05

Mobile

The same content is designed for a 375px screen: sections stack into a single column, the service cards follow one another, and the navigation collapses so the page stays readable on a phone.
